Contrastive learning
● Skipgram with negative sampling is an instance of contrastive learning
○ Data is pairs (x,y) that are similar to each other
○ You can assume that random pairs should be dissimilar
● Very popular nowadays for representation learning in vision (CLIP) and 
for information retrieval (DPR)

--- PAGE 112 ---
Word Representations: Summary
● Words are central to language
● Graph-based representations are difﬁcult to manipulate and compose
● Vector representations for words are useful and efficient, encoding semantic similarity as 
inner product similarity.
● One-hot vectors: useful in some contexts but not all
● Word2vec: learn representations from data with machine learning
○ Idea: self-supervised learning for learning from a lot of raw data
○ Idea: contrastive learning for improved efficiency with negative sampling
○ Idea: can be viewed as implicit matrix factorization 
● GloVe: combine self-supervised learning view with matrix factorization view
● Evaluating word representations: intrinsic, extrinsic; challenging and open-ended
