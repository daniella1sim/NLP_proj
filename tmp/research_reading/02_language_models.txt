--- PAGE 63 ---
Unknown words
● What about unknown words at test time?
● Solution: use a special token <UNK>
○ Fix the vocabulary only with words appearing more than k times 
○ Replace the rest with <UNK> and train
○ At test time, replace with <UNK> any word that is not in V
● Advanced solutions
○ Backoff methods (use progressively shorter histories)
○ Smoothing (“free-up” probability mass for unknown words)

--- PAGE 64 ---
Language Modeling: Summary So Far
● Language model: 
○ Assigning a probability to every sentence
○ Using conditional probabilities, we get a generative model that produces a 
probability distribution for the next word
● Evaluation: intrinsic evaluation with perplexity, extrinsic evaluation with 
downstream task & further fine-tuning (lecture 5)
● N-gram models:
○ Decompose text probability with the chain rule and use a Markov 
assumption: limited ”memory”
○ Smooth estimates to do better on rare events
○ Work surprisingly okay but leave a lot to be desired
○ Many attempts to improve LMs using syntax but not easy
○ Many improvements: interpolation, supporting unknown words, …
