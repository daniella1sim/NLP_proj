LRMs - OpenAI o1
“Our large-scale reinforcement learning algorithm teaches the model how to think productively using its 
chain of thought in a highly data-eﬃcient training process. We have found that the performance of o1 
consistently improves with more reinforcement learning (train-time compute) and with more time spent 
thinking (test-time compute). The constraints on scaling this approach differ substantially from those of 
LLM pretraining, and we are continuing to investigate them. ”
Learning to reason with LLMs, OpenAI o1, September 2024 34

Conclusion - part 1
Reasoning failures: LLMs cannot necessarily reason “out of the box” (i.e., solve complex tasks)

inference-time compute, either sequentially (reasoning models) or parallelly (majority sampling)

AutoResearch
90
karpathy/autoresearch: The idea: give an AI agent a small but real LLM training setup and let it experiment 
autonomously overnight. It modiﬁes the code, trains for 5 minutes, checks if the result improved, keeps or 
discards, and repeats. You wake up in the morning to a log of experiments and (hopefully) a better model.
