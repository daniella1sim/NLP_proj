balance scaling up data vs. scaling up parameters?

the model size and the number of training 
tokens should be scaled equally: for every 
doubling of model size the number of 
training tokens should also be doubled”

--- PAGE 37 ---
Scaling Up the Architecture: Mixture of Expert (MoE)
● MoE is an architecture that increases the number of model parameters with the 
same amount of compute
● Increasingly popular in very large models
● Idea: every input activates only some model parameters
○ Meaning, only a small subset of the parameters are used to compute output
○ All parameters are used for some inputs (so no “dead” parameters, ideally)
● How?
○ Different parts of the networks will become “experts” in certain types of inputs
○ Each input will be routed to the few appropriate “expert” parts of the network

--- PAGE 43 ---
MoE: Pros and Cons
● Pros:
○ Gains in performance per compute (if done right)
○ Enables expert parallelism 
○ Natural way to distribute a model across machines
● Cons:
○ Difficult to train
■ Routing is a discrete decision
■ Need specialized losses to avoid expert imbalance or “dead experts”
○ Architecturally complex
○ “Worth it” only at very large scales

--- PAGE 61 ---
Speculative Decoding
Idea: Use small and large models
● Smaller model quickly ”drafts” a few tokens
○ Sequential generation, but with small model
● Larger model verifies drafts:
○ Rejects tokens that aren’t good enough
■ Verification is parallel!
○ Generates only at first rejected token
■ Sequential generation of much fewer tokens with large model
● Repeat (few tokens at a time)
Xia et al., 2022; Leviathan et al., 2022; Chen et al., 2023

Conclusion
● Scale is one of the key factors in the success of LLMs
● We saw various tuning approaches to “adjust” large models
● Scaling has multiple facets, including data, parameters, and 
inference-time compute
● Lots of approaches for how to scale along these axes. We saw tools 
that give performance estimations and measure the tradeoffs.
● Efficiency considerations are important, we saw a few ideas but there 
are many more (e.g. distillation and latent reasoning architectures)
